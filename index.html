Status Pocket Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: Status Pocket does not collect, store, or share your personal data. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Everything stays on your phone.

About the app

Status Pocket (package name app.statuspocket) is an Android app that lets you save photo and video statuses you have already viewed in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business to your own device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app does not have a server, does not create an account for you, and does not send any data anywhere. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot transmit data off your device.

Files and folder access

To show you viewed statuses, the app asks you to choose a folder using Android’s built-in folder picker. This is the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business media folder on your device.

  • The app can only read the folder you choose and approve.
  • Android remembers your choice so you do not have to pick the folder each time. You can revoke it at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • When you tap download, the selected photo or video is copied to your device’s own storage. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Files are only read to display thumbnails and previews, and to save the ones you choose.

Third parties

Status Pocket does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or any other third-party services, and does not share data with anyone. Status Pocket is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.

Your content

Statuses belong to the people who posted them. Please save and use content only when you have permission from its owner, and respect other people’s privacy and copyright.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you. Files you save remain on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes the app and its settings, but files you already saved stay in your gallery until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
